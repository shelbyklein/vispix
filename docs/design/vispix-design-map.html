In use: full 102 · lg 98 · xl 77 · md 76 · sm 26 · 2xl 8. Chips and avatars are pills, cards are lg–xl, buttons and inputs are md.

Elevation · flat by default

shadow-sm → lg. Surfaces mostly rely on a 1px border and a slightly lighter card color; shadows are reserved for buttons (xs) and overlays.

Spacing

Tailwind's 4px scale. Gaps cluster at gap-1–gap-4 (4–16px), cards pad p-6 (24px) in the primitive, but most screens override to tighter values. Content width is usually full-bleed, with max-w-2xl / max-w-7xl on settings-style pages.

Known gaps

Inconsistencies found while mapping. Worth deciding on before or during a redesign.

ImpactGapDetail
HighButtons and badges have no hover statebutton.tsx and badge.tsx rely on hover-elevate / active-elevate-2 classes (a leftover from the Replit template), but those utilities are never defined; only their --elevate-* variables exist in index.css. Most variants also had their own hover removed.
MediumStatus colors bypass the tokensAmber and emerald are hard-coded Tailwind colors (~270 uses), so they can't be themed and the dark-mode variants are handled ad hoc per component. A --warning / --success token pair would fix this.
MediumPage headers are copy-pasted~16 pages repeat their own h1 markup with mixed sizes (text-2xl vs text-xl); a shared PageHeader would make title, subtitle and actions consistent.
MediumHeading classes are misleadingPages set text-foreground on h1, but the base layer forces the heading color with !important, so the class has no effect.
LowMany radius valuesfull, lg, xl, md, sm and 2xl are all common; there's no rule for which surface gets which.
LowLocally edited shadcn primitives// @replit edits make upgrading from shadcn harder; worth documenting or reverting when touching them.
LowSerif body fallback is unused--app-font-serif (Georgia) is defined but headings use --app-font-heading; nothing uses the serif token.
